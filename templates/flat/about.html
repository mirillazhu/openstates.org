About This Site

This is a version of the Open States legislative information platform that has been specifically designed to meet specifications for prison and jail tablets. Using the site, individuals can find out who represents them, follow bills about topics they care about, read the text of pending and passed bills, and access related bill documents, votes history, and committee data. It is our hope that this information can support the extraordinary (but at the same time, everyday and ordinary) civic engagement and policy building taking place within facilities and make it easier to participate in the legislative process so that our democracy can be truly accessible to all.

Code forked from the openstates.org repository of the Open States project. Major thanks to the Open States developers and maintainers for their support in making this endeavor possible, as well as to the contributors of all the libraries this site depends on.

About Open States

Open States strives to improve civic engagement by providing data and tools to help people understand the legislative process that creates public policy. Open States is the leading source of standardized legislative data across state borders in the United States, since the project's founding in 2009. The project aims to serve members of the public, community groups, journalists, and researchers with better data on policy-making, and to provide tools to reduce barriers to democratic participation.
